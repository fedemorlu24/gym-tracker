# Gym Tracker

**Am I getting stronger?**  
A free, offline-first workout diary that helps you find out.

[**Open Gym Tracker →**](https://fedemorlu24.github.io/gym-tracker/)  
On your phone, tap **Add to Home Screen** to install.

No account, subscriptions or ads. Your data stays on your device.

## Features

- **Log workouts quickly** — Track sets, reps and weight with simple buttons. See your previous performance, get progression suggestions and use the built-in rest timer.
- **Follow your program** — Create a plan, receive one from a coach or use the beginner full-body routine. Import plans from photos or PDFs with help from an AI app, or import workout history from CSV/TSV files.
- **Track your progress** — View strength and weight charts, personal records, weekly comparisons, goals and streaks.
- **Estimate muscle recovery** — See a colour-coded body map based on training volume, effort and age. Recovery scores are **estimates, not measurements**.
- **Track your body** — Log body weight, calories and protein, and compare progress photos stored on your device.
- **Explore exercises** — Browse 131 movements and 485 variants, with target muscles, execution tips and suggested rep ranges.
- **Share your achievements** — Send workout cards, progress images and challenges through links or WhatsApp.

Available in **Italian, English, Spanish, French and German**, with light and dark themes. Works fully offline.

## Keep your data safe

Export and restore your data with **JSON backups**. Data is tied to the app’s URL: keep using the same address and **export a backup before removing the app or clearing browser data**.
